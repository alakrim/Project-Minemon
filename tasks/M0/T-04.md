## Goal and why
Let the owner move through the world and see fps and memory, so the M0 performance gate can be measured on their PC.

## Read
CLAUDE.md, docs/CONVENTIONS.md, docs/PLAN.md (Invariant 4, Performance gate), project.godot (to check the action names), game/settings.gd (stub).

## Interfaces / invariants
- **Player** (`entities/player.tscn` with `entities/player.gd`): a CharacterBody3D with a capsule collider (height 1.8 m, radius 0.3) and a child `Camera3D` named `Camera` at y 1.62.
  - Mouse-look uses `Settings.mouse_sensitivity`, with pitch clamped to ±89°. The mouse is captured when gameplay starts and released on `menu`.
  - Walking 4.3 m/s, sprinting 5.6 m/s, jump velocity 8, gravity 25.
  - `toggle_fly` toggles fly mode: no gravity, 10 m/s, `jump` goes up and `dodge` goes down.
  - `@export var active: bool = true`. When false, the player ignores input and physics; T-05 enables it after spawn.
  - Reads input only through InputMap actions.
- **Debug overlay** (`ui/debug_overlay.tscn` with `.gd`): a CanvasLayer containing one Label, toggled with `toggle_debug` and visible by default. It refreshes every 0.25 s with lines starting exactly `FPS:`, `Frame ms:`, `Memory MB:` (`Performance.MEMORY_STATIC`), `Draw calls:` (`Performance.RENDER_TOTAL_DRAW_CALLS_IN_FRAME`), `Renderer:` (`RenderingServer.get_current_rendering_method()`), `GPU:` (`RenderingServer.get_video_adapter_name()`) and `Pos:`. It has `func set_extra(key:String, value:String)` for extra lines (T-05 uses it for chunk counts).
- **Settings:** replace the body of `game/settings.gd` (keep `extends Node`).
  - Values: `view_radius:int=4`, `fullscreen:bool=false`, `mouse_sensitivity:float=0.002`.
  - `load_settings()` runs in `_ready()` and `save_settings()` writes `user://settings.json`.
  - A missing or corrupt file, or missing keys, fall back to the defaults without crashing.
  - `func set_path_for_tests(p:String)` lets tests use a temporary path.
- **Environment** (`world/environment.tscn`): a Node3D containing:
  - a WorldEnvironment with background sky colour #87a9c9, depth fog enabled (density 0.012, colour #9fb4c7) and ambient light (colour #c8d4e0, energy 0.6);
  - a DirectionalLight3D with shadows off (rotation −50° X, 30° Y).

  T-05 instances it under Main/World.
