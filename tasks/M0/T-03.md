# T-03 — Voxel world: blocks, column chunks, terrain, threaded mesher, streamer

## Goal and why
Build a blocky world that streams in around the player and is fast on an AMD Vega 8 iGPU. This is the project's main performance risk (D-015, D-017).

## Read
CLAUDE.md, docs/CONVENTIONS.md, docs/PLAN.md (the Chunk, ChunkSnapshot, ChunkMesher, TerrainGenerator and WorldStreamer interfaces; Invariants 2 and 6; Performance gate), core/rng.gd.

## Interfaces / invariants
Implement exactly the PLAN.md signatures. In addition:
- **BlockRegistry** (world/block_registry.gd, RefCounted): `load_from(path:String)->Array[String]` (errors), `color(id:int)->Color`, `is_solid(id)->bool`, `is_transparent(id)->bool`.
  - data/blocks.json ids: 0 air (transparent), 1 grass, 2 dirt, 3 stone, 4 sand, 5 water (transparent, not solid), 6 log, 7 leaves (transparent), 8 snow.
- **Chunk:** a 16×128×16 column; index = x + z·16 + y·256. Out-of-range get returns 0, and out-of-range set is ignored.
- **ChunkSnapshot:**
  - `_init(c:Chunk, north:Chunk, south:Chunk, east:Chunk, west:Chunk)`, where neighbours may be null (null means air);
  - copies the blocks with `PackedByteArray.duplicate()` and the 4 border slices;
  - `get_block(p:Vector3i)` works for x and z in −1..16.
- **ChunkMesher.build** returns **ChunkMeshData** (world/chunk_mesh_data.gd, RefCounted) with `vertices:PackedVector3Array, normals:PackedVector3Array, colors:PackedColorArray, indices:PackedInt32Array, face_count:int`.
  - A face is emitted only when the neighbour is air or transparent and differs from the block itself (water next to water emits no face).
  - Face shade: top 1.0, sides 0.8, bottom 0.6, multiplied by the block colour.
- **TerrainGenerator:**
  - seeded `FastNoiseLite` (simplex, frequency 0.004); height = 20 + noise·70 clamped to 1..100; sea level 32 (water fills below it);
  - biome noise (frequency 0.0015) selects plains, forest, mountain or beach: sand near sea level, snow above 80, trees (log 4–6 high plus a leaves blob) in forest at a deterministic 1 per ~40 surface blocks, decided with `Rng.new(seed).fork("tree:%d,%d" % [x, z])`;
  - `surface_height(x, z)` returns the y of the top solid block without building a chunk.
- **WorldStreamer:**
  - On `set_focus`, it queues columns within `view_radius` (Chebyshev distance in columns) that are not loaded, and unloads columns beyond `view_radius+1`.
  - Jobs run on `WorkerThreadPool`: the worker generates the chunk; the main thread makes the snapshot; the worker meshes it.
  - Results are applied on the main thread in `_process`, at most 4 columns per frame: an ArrayMesh with a single `StandardMaterial3D` (vertex colour as albedo, `shading_mode` per-pixel) and one MeshInstance3D per column. A `ConcavePolygonShape3D` StaticBody3D is built only within `collision_radius`.
  - When a neighbour loads, it re-meshes the affected edge column(s).
  - `flush()` waits for every queued task (`WorkerThreadPool.wait_for_task_completion`) and applies every pending result. It is used by tests and at spawn.
  - `loaded_count()` counts columns with an applied mesh. `has_collision_at(pos)` is true when the column containing pos has collision.

## Steps
1. Write world/block_registry.gd, chunk.gd, chunk_snapshot.gd, chunk_mesh_data.gd, chunk_mesher.gd, terrain_generator.gd, world_streamer.gd and data/blocks.json.
2. Write tests in tests/unit/test_world_*.gd:
   - chunk get/set, index and bounds;
   - one solid block gives face_count 6, two adjacent blocks give 10, and water next to water gives no shared face;
   - a snapshot with neighbours culls the faces on the border;
   - generator determinism (same seed and position give an identical PackedByteArray; seeds 1 and 2 differ);
   - `surface_height` equals the top solid block of the generated chunk at 5 sample points;
   - a streamer in a headless scene with view_radius 1: after `set_focus(Vector3.ZERO)` and `flush()`, `loaded_count()==9` and `has_collision_at(Vector3.ZERO)` is true.
3. Write `tools/bench_world.gd` (run with `$GODOT --headless --path . -s tools/bench_world.gd`). It generates and meshes 81 columns, then prints the average and maximum ms per column and the total face count. It is not part of check.sh.
