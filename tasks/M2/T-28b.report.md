# T-28b report: spawn sanctuary placement retry; free ports (builder)

Root cause: `find_ground` took its candidates from `core.get_spawn_level`, which is the mapgen's height estimate, not the real nodes. In the test world the estimate was nil or an air level, so `floor_ok` never passed. The old unchecked fallback had hidden this.

Fix (structure.lua, init.lua):
- `find_ground` scans the real loaded columns for a floor whose full 9 x 9 is dry solid. The rings widen per attempt (`radii_for`: 8 to 48).
- `spawn_attempt(n)` drives the retries. The globalstep keeps retrying every 5 s with no cap.
- From attempt 6, a failed search lays `foundation()`: a 3 x 3 stone pillar down to solid ground (48 deep), or a 3 x 3 platform.

Test: drives the attempts, and checks the radii and the foundation.

Ports: `run_luanti_test.sh` and `screenshot.sh` pick a random free port in 30000-39999 with a python3 UDP bind.

`bash scripts/check.sh`: exit 0 twice (398 busted, 13 integration).
