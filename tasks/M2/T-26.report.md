# T-26 report: minemon_capture (builder)

Built `mods/minemon/minemon_capture/`: four balls (Poké, Great, Ultra, Master) with recipes and generated icons, the left-click throw, the shake sequence, catch to party or PC, break-free, the odds HUD, the starter kit and `/give_balls`. Runtime: `creature_defeated` on exhaustion, `break_free` (enraged 30 s) and its export. `bash scripts/check.sh`: exit 0 (CHECK PASSED, capture suite 47 checks).

Deviations for Opus review:
1. Exhausted creatures sit at 0 HP, so a catch joins at max(1, hp), not hp.
2. VoxeLibre has no iron nugget; the Poké Ball recipe uses gold nugget.
3. `ASSETS_LICENSES.md` is outside the allowed files, so the four ball PNG rows are not added (row text below).
4. At 0 HP a catch_rate 255 species (Rattata) has catch chance 1, so it always catches and never breaks free. Design call needed.
5. Party full and no PC: the capture is undone and the ball returned.

## In-game checks
- Throw: arc at about 22 m/s; a bounce on a wild non-exhausted creature says "It's too strong — weaken it first!" and it turns on the thrower; a node hit drops the ball; throw sound plays.
- Catch: "Gotcha! <Name> was caught!" in gold, the party grows, the chime plays.
- Failure: "Oh no! <Name> broke free!" in red, the ball is gone, the creature stays hostile for 30 s, then calms to its temperament.
- Odds HUD: "Catch ~NN%" green at 60+, yellow at 25+, red below, shown only while a ball is wielded and an exhausted wild creature is in the look ray.
- Crafting: Poké Ball x2 (gold nugget, redstone, gold nugget); Great Ball = Poké Ball + lapis; Ultra Ball = Poké Ball + gold ingot + obsidian; Master Ball has no recipe. Tooltip shows "Catch rate ×N".
- Starter kit: 5 Poké Balls once, on join or 2 s after the first send-out.
