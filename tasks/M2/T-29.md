# T-29 — Move command bar: HUD with 4 moves, Z cycles, Party Ball click orders the selected move
ID / milestone / wave / risk: T-29 / M2 / wave 2 / medium

## Goal and why
Real-time battles need player skill, not just AI. D-026 promised manual move choice in M2, and D-031 sets the controls. The player keeps the AI as a fallback but can call the exact move at the right moment: Ember on the Grass type, or Sleep Powder before the capture throw.

CLAUDE.md, docs/CONVENTIONS.md, docs/DECISIONS.md D-026, D-028, D-031, docs/research/S-15-m2w2-shortcuts.md (§2), mods/minemon/minemon_party/{init.lua, brain.lua, ui.lua, party_logic.lua}, mods/minemon/minemon_combat/init.lua (`use_move`, `choose_move`, `on_cooldown`), and minemon_core data moves.json and types.json (type colours if present; otherwise reuse the colours used by the starter chooser).

## Interfaces / invariants
- **HUD bar** (bottom centre, above the hotbar; keep the VoxeLibre health and hunger bars clear) is shown only while the player has an active creature. It has 4 slots. Each slot shows:
  - the move name (short), a type colour chip and "PP n/max";
  - a cooldown dim when `on_cooldown`.

  The selected slot has a bright frame. Update it at most every 0.25 s and on change. Remove it when the creature is recalled or faints.
- **Cycle:** the Z key (`get_player_control().zoom`, edge-detected in a globalstep) selects the next move with PP above 0. Sneak plus left click with the Party Ball pointing at nothing also cycles. The selection is per player (Lua table) and resets to slot 1 on send-out. It shows a short chat-free HUD flash of the move name.
- **Order:** left click with the Party Ball on a valid foe target sets the ordered target (existing behaviour) **and** queues the selected move. The owned brain casts the queued move as soon as it is in range and off cooldown (use `minemon.combat.use_move`), then clears the queue and goes back to AI choice.
  - A queued move expires after 6 s.
  - Show "<Name>, use <Move>!" on order.
  - If the move is out of PP: "No PP left for <Move>!" and nothing is queued.
- **Self-target moves** (archetype aura, or effect target user): if the selected move targets the user, left click on air with the ball (no sneak) casts it at once, without a target.
- **Brain change:** in minemon_party/brain.lua, before the AI `choose_move` step, check the queue. Keep everything else in the owned brain unchanged.
- Put the pure parts (cycle skipping 0-PP moves, queue expiry, self-target detection) in `party_logic.lua` or a new `command_logic.lua`.
