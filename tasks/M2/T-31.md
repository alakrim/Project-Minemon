# T-31 — Status-move pass: stat stages, sleep/paralysis/confusion/flinch, leech seed, protect, healing, the remaining no-op moves
ID / milestone / wave / risk: T-31 / M2 / wave 2 / medium

## Goal and why
BACKLOG: 43 status moves currently do nothing, and flinch, confusion, trap, protect and leech seed are ignored (T-23). Choosing moves (T-29) only matters if every move does something. Fights need tactics: put the foe to sleep, then throw the ball.

CLAUDE.md, docs/CONVENTIONS.md, docs/DECISIONS.md D-003, D-026, D-031, mods/minemon/minemon_combat/** (init.lua `use_move`, `apply_stage`, `apply_status`, status.lua, status_logic.lua, moves_logic.lua, archetypes.lua, choose_logic.lua), minemon_core/data/moves.json (each move's `effect` object), minemon_core/formulas.lua (damage view stages), and the T-23 report (tasks/M1/T-23.report.md) for what exists.

## Interfaces / invariants
- **First, inventory:** list every move in moves.json whose `effect` (or category status) is not handled today, and write that list in the report. Group the moves by mechanic.
- **Mechanics to implement**, real-time adapted. Keep the durations in seconds, since there are no turns:
  - **Stat stages** −6..+6 for atk/def/spa/spd/spe/accuracy/evasion. They are already in the damage view? Verify, and make sure every move with `stat_changes` applies them (user or target), stages decay toward 0 by 1 every 20 s, and they reset on recall.
  - **Sleep:** 3–7 s; no moves or movement; the sit animation; damage does not wake it, as in Gen 2.
  - **Paralysis:** speed ×0.5, and a 25% chance per cast to fail with "is paralyzed!".
  - **Confusion:** 4–8 s; each cast has a 33% chance to hit itself (typeless power 40) instead.
  - **Flinch:** the next cast within 1 s is cancelled.
  - **Trap** (wrap, bind, mean look): the target cannot flee or be recalled for 6 s, and wraps deal 1/16 max HP per second.
  - **Leech seed:** 1/8 max HP every 2 s to the seeder for 15 s; grass types are immune.
  - **Protect/detect:** 1.5 s of immunity, with consecutive use halving the success chance.
  - **Endure:** survive at 1 HP during 1.5 s.
  - **Healing moves** (recover, softboiled, milk drink, rest, moonlight, synthesis, morning sun): heal by fraction; rest = full heal plus 5 s of sleep; the sun moves heal 2/3 during the day and 1/4 at night.
  - **Haze** resets all stages within 6 m. **Mist** blocks stat drops for 10 s. **Light screen** and **reflect** halve special or physical damage to the user for 10 s. **Focus energy** gives a crit bonus. **Fixed damage** (night shade and seismic toss = level; dragon rage 40; sonic boom 20; super fang half HP; psywave; endeavor). **OHKO moves** (fissure, guillotine, horn drill): accuracy 30, fail against a higher level.
  - **Anything left** (metronome, mimic, transform, baton pass, conversion and so on): either a simple real-time version or an explicit "no effect yet" list in the report. Do not spend long on edge moves.
- **Visible feedback** for each status: the existing status particles/tint plus floating text through `G.feedback_near` ("fell asleep!", "is confused!", "protected itself!"). The nametag gets a status suffix if one exists.
- **Pure logic** goes in status_logic.lua or a new `effects_logic.lua`. Keep `use_move`'s signature and contract unchanged.
- The AI (choose_logic.lua) should value status moves sensibly: no sleep move on an already sleeping target, healing below 40% HP, stat-ups early in a fight. Keep this light.
