# T-28 — minemon_sanctuary: Sanctuary (Pokémon Center) with healing altar + respawn, warding, PC box, shop, coins, potions
ID / milestone / wave / risk: T-28 / M2 / wave 1 / medium

## Goal and why
The night is horrific. **Sanctuaries** are ancient lantern shrines, the only places the dark will not enter (D-030). Here the player heals the party and binds their respawn point. Each one also has a PC for storing creatures and a keeper's counter that sells items for ₽ Coins, which defeated wild creatures drop. This turns the world into a "safe light vs. hostile dark" loop, like the bonfires in Dark Souls.

## Interfaces / invariants
- **New mod `minemon_sanctuary`.** Depends on minemon_core, minemon_creatures, minemon_party and mcl_spawn, with mcl_formspec and mcl_sounds optional. Pure logic goes in `sanctuary_logic.lua` and `pc_logic.lua`.
- **Nodes** (our textures generated by tools/gen_textures.py or a new tools/gen_sanctuary_textures.py, stdlib only; style: weathered stone, pale gold, warm light):
  - `minemon_sanctuary:altar`: the healing altar; light 14, unbreakable in survival;
  - `minemon_sanctuary:pc`: a terminal;
  - `minemon_sanctuary:counter`: the keeper's counter;
  - `minemon_sanctuary:lantern`: light 14, decorative, and it marks the ward.
- **Structure:** `minemon.sanctuary.place(pos)` builds a small shrine with code (not a schematic file): a 9×9 stone floor, pillars, 4 lanterns, an altar in the centre, a PC and a counter on the sides, an open roof frame, and air cleared inside. Register the sanctuary centre in mod storage (a list of positions).
  - **Spawn sanctuary:** on the first player join of a world (mod storage flag), place one sanctuary on the ground about 8 nodes from the world spawn point. Find solid ground; avoid water if possible within 32 nodes.
  - `/sanctuary` (privilege server) places one at the player's position.
- **Altar** (right click):
  - heal the whole party (hp to max, status none, pp refilled; reuse `minemon.party.heal_all` and extend it if pp is not refilled);
  - recall the active creature first;
  - set the respawn: `mcl_spawn.set_player_spawn_pos(player, pos_above_altar, true, true)`. Check that respawn there works with the bed setting both on and off, and test the function call in integration;
  - show golden particles and a chime: "Your party is restored. The lanterns will remember you.".
- **Warding:** `minemon.sanctuary.is_safe(pos)` returns true within 16 nodes (horizontal; vertical within 8) of any sanctuary centre. Minimal edits in minemon_creatures:
  - in `spawning.lua`, no wild spawns within 24 nodes;
  - in `wild_brain.lua`, wild creatures whose target (player or owned creature) is inside a ward drop the target and walk away. A wild creature inside a ward is non-aggressive.

  Keep both edits tiny and guarded with `if minemon.sanctuary and ...`.
- **PC:**
  - API `minemon.pc.deposit(player, inst) -> bool`, `minemon.pc.list(player)`, `minemon.pc.withdraw(player, index) -> bool` (fails when the party is full). Storage is player meta `minemon:pc:list` (JSON, serialized instances, cap 240). T-26 calls `deposit` when the party is full.
  - Formspec at the PC node: party column (6 rows) and box pages (30 per page, 8 pages), showing species name, Lv and nickname. Click a party row then "Deposit"; click a box entry then "Withdraw". Depositing the last party member with HP above 0 is refused ("You can't go out there alone.").
- **Coins:** `minemon:coin` ("₽ Coin", our generated icon, stack_max 999).
  - On `creature_defeated(victim_le, attacker)`, drop coins at the victim's position: `amount = ceil(level/3) + rng(0..2)`, doubled at night (night is risky and rewarding).
  - T-26 adds the event fire in parallel; tests fire the event themselves.
- **Shop** (counter right click): a formspec listing every items.json entry with price > 0 where `core.registered_items["minemon:"..id]` exists. Prices are in coins: the coin price = items.json price / 100, rounded up, at least 1. Buy ×1 or ×5. The player's coin count is shown and coins are taken from the inventory. Items not yet registered are hidden.
- **Potions:** register `minemon:potion` and `minemon:super_potion` (heal from items.json; our icons). Right click heals the active creature, or else the first injured party member that is not fainted, then consumes the potion: "<Name> recovered N HP.".
- **Starter supplies:** once per player (meta flag `minemon:sanctuary:kit`), when the party is non-empty: 3 Potions and 10 coins. Also show a chat hint once: "Follow the lanterns. Sanctuaries keep the night away.".
