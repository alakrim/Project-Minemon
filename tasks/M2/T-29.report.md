# T-29 report (builder)

Built the move command bar in `minemon_party`: a 4-slot HUD bar (name, type chip, PP n/max, cooldown dim, selected frame) while a creature is out. Z and sneak + left click on air cycle the selected move (skips 0 PP, wraps). A left click on a foe with the Party Ball orders it and queues the selected move; the owned brain casts it in reach and off cooldown, expiring after 6 s. An air click casts a self-target move at once. Pure rules are in `command_logic.lua`.

Deviations: the Party Ball's `on_use` is replaced in `command.lua` (`core.override_item`), so `ui.lua` is untouched; T-30 must not change that `on_use`. `S.order` takes an optional move name. No non-aura move has only user-target stat changes, so that self-target clause matches nothing today.
