# T-30 — Dread variants: night-corrupted eldritch creatures, Dread Shards, Umbral Ball
ID / milestone / wave / risk: T-30 / M2 / wave 2 / high (touches spawning, runtime visuals and the instance format)

## Goal and why
The night must be horrific, and strong creatures should feel like eldritch beasts (owner goal). **Dread variants** are rare corrupted forms that only walk at night. They are hunted for their shards and caught as trophies. This is our horror take on shiny hunting, and a grind loop (D-031).

## Interfaces / invariants
- **Instance:** add an optional boolean `dread` to the instance (default false). Add it to the `SCALARS` list in instance.lua, so serialize and deserialize keep it; old saves load as false. Add a unit test for the round trip.
- **Spawning roll** (pure function in a new `dread_logic.lua` in minemon_creatures): a wild spawn becomes Dread only at night (VoxeLibre timeofday < 0.23 or > 0.77).
  - Chance = 1/40 near spawn, rising linearly to 1/12 at 1500+ nodes from origin.
  - Never inside a Sanctuary ward (`minemon.sanctuary.is_safe`).
  - Dread level = normal wild level + 5..12 (cap 100).
- **Dread stats:** calc_stats ×1.25 for hp/atk/spa/spe while `dread` is true. Apply this in the one place where stats are computed for creatures: add an optional multiplier in `minemon.instance.stats`, keyed on `inst.dread`, so owned Dread creatures keep it, at ×1.10 when owned.
- **Behaviour:** a wild Dread creature is aggressive whatever its species temperament: it hunts the nearest player or owned creature within 20 nodes. Use the existing wild brain's aggressive path by overriding the temperament lookup for Dread entities with a minimal guarded edit in wild_brain.lua or wild_logic.lua. The aggression gate still respects Sanctuary wards.
- **Look** (runtime.lua or a new `dread_fx.lua` called on activation; staticdata already carries `_mm`):
  - texture mod `^[multiply:#5a3a6a^[colorize:#200010:90` or similar dark violet-black (tune it with screenshots);
  - `glow = 6`;
  - an attached particle spawner of dark smoke, sparse and drifting up, with a few deep red glints;
  - nametag "Dread <Name> Lv N" in colour #B0102A.
  - The cry or hurt sound is played at pitch 0.6 (use the existing sound with a pitch parameter).
  - When a Dread creature first comes within 16 nodes of a player at night, play a low VoxeLibre ambient sting once per creature for that player (cave or ambient sound).
- **Dawn:** a wild (unowned) Dread creature, not exhausted and not mid-capture, when timeofday enters day (0.23..0.77), bursts into smoke particles and is removed with `mcl_util.remove_entity`, with a hiss. "It cannot bear the light."
- **Rewards** (listen to `creature_defeated` in your new mod `minemon_dread`, or in minemon_creatures if simpler):
  - drop 1..2 `minemon:dread_shard` (our generated icon: a black-violet jagged crystal, via tools/gen_icons.py in a separate function);
  - coins ×3 for Dread victims: make the sanctuary coin-drop function multiply by 3 when `victim_le._mm.dread`. This is a minimal edit in minemon_sanctuary/init.lua.
- **Umbral Ball** `minemon:umbral_ball`:
  - catch multiplier 3.0 against Dread creatures and 0.5 otherwise;
  - recipe: Ultra Ball + 4 Dread Shards (shapeless);
  - our generated icon, black with a violet band;
  - register it in minemon_capture (minimal edit: ball table plus a multiplier hook `capture_logic.ball_mult(ball_id, inst)`).

  Normal balls get ×0.75 against Dread creatures. Add `umbral_ball` to items.json (kind ball, price 0: not sold).
- **Captured Dread:** keeps `dread=true` and starts with bond 20 (not 70). The tint and smoke persist when it is sent out (lighter smoke). The party HUD and formspec show a "☠" mark.
