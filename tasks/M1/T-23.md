# T-23 — minemon_combat: real-time moves (6 archetypes), status effects, move choice, VFX
ID / milestone / wave / risk: T-23 / M1 / wave 2 / high (core gameplay)

## Goal and why
Creatures fight in real time in the world (D-003, D-026). Every move in moves.json must work through one of 6 archetypes, with readable type-coloured effects, Gen 2 damage and type effectiveness.

## Interfaces / invariants
- **Mod `minemon_combat`:** depends = minemon_core, minemon_creatures, vl_projectile, mcl_util.
- **API (contract):** `minemon.combat.use_move(user_obj, target_obj, move_id)` returns true or `false, reason`. Also `minemon.combat.choose_move(user_obj, target_obj)` returns a move_id or nil, and `minemon.combat.on_cooldown(user_obj, move_id)` returns a bool.
- **Damage application:**
  - always call `minemon.creatures.damage(target, amount, user, {move_id, effectiveness, crit})` when it exists (T-24 adds it in parallel);
  - otherwise use the fallback `mcl_util.deal_damage(target, amount, {type="mob", source=user})`.
  - Players as targets: amount_player = clamp(floor(amount / 4), 1, 8), applied with `target:punch(user, 1, {full_punch_interval=1, damage_groups={fleshy=amount_player}})`.
- **Damage math:** `minemon.formulas.damage(atk_view, def_view, move, minemon.data.type_multiplier, rng)`. Views come from the instance (`minemon.instance.stats`), with `stages` kept on the luaentity `_mm_stages` (−6..6; reset on recall).
- **Use rules:**
  - the move must be in the user's instance moves (wild users have unlimited PP; owned users decrement `pp` and cannot use a move at 0 PP);
  - per-move cooldown `cooldown_s`, kept on the luaentity;
  - cast time `cast_s` with a wind-up particle on the user, after which the target is re-validated (it still exists and is in range);
  - the user cannot act while exhausted, asleep or frozen; paralysis gives a 25% fail chance.
- **Archetypes:**
  - **projectile:** a `vl_projectile`-registered entity `minemon_combat:orb` (S-12 §5; always pass a damage_groups TABLE: gotcha 3) at about 14 m/s with a type-coloured particle trail. It hits the first creature or player that is not the user or its owner, then deals damage.
  - **beam:** an instant raycast (`core.raycast`) from the user's eye to the target within range_m. Particles along the line. It hits the first object on the line (not the user); blocks stop it.
  - **melee_arc:** the target within range_m (≤ 3.5) and in front of the user (dot > 0.3). A lunge or punch animation and a swoosh of particles.
  - **area_burst:** every creature or player within radius = range_m around the target position (or the user, if the move targets self or all), except the user and its owner's own creatures, with linear falloff to 50% at the edge. A particle sphere.
  - **aura:** applies `effect.stat_changes` to self (or the target if `target:"target"`), with glow particles.
  - **status_cloud:** a lingering particle cloud for 3 s at the target that applies `effect.status` with `status_chance` to entities inside, checked every 0.5 s.
- **Status effects** (the minimal set): burn and poison deal 1/16 max HP every 2 s; paralysis gives 25% move fail and −50% speed; sleep (2–5 s, and wakes on damage) and freeze (until hit by fire, or 10% per second to thaw) block moves. Keep a bounded registry of affected objects, ticked by one globalstep every 0.5 s, with dead or removed objects pruned.
- **choose_move (pure logic in choose_logic.lua):** score each off-cooldown move with PP as expected damage = power × accuracy × STAB × type multiplier, with status moves scored at 30 if the target has no status and 0 otherwise. Pick the best, with 15% randomness from the rng.
- **VFX textures:** generated by a Python stdlib addition to tools/gen_standins.py (or a new tools/gen_vfx.py): 8×8 soft orb, spark and puff PNGs in white, tinted per type with `^[multiply:#rrggbb`. They are our own art.
- **Effectiveness feedback:** for players within 24 m of the target, a short HUD text near the crosshair for 1.2 s: "Super effective!" (green), "Not very effective…" (grey), "No effect" (dark), "Critical!" (gold). Rate-limit it to one per 0.5 s per player.
- **Performance:** at most one globalstep (status ticking every 0.5 s), and at most 8 particles per spawner call. Projectiles remove themselves after 3 s.
