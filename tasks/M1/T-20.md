## Interfaces / invariants
- **Files:** `minemon_core/init.lua` (glue: create `minemon`, load data, expose modules), `data.lua` (data registry; `core.parse_json` is used only in init.lua, so pass the parsed tables to data.lua to keep it pure), `formulas.lua`, `rng.lua`, `instance.lua`, `events.lua`.
- **Global declaration:** use `minemon = rawget(_G, "minemon") or {}` to avoid the strict-mode "undeclared global" warning (verifier finding).
- **Formulas:** the Gen 2 rules from the Godot port.
  - **Stats:**
    - HP = floor((2·base+iv)·L/100) + L + 10;
    - others = floor((2·base+iv)·L/100) + 5;
    - then × (1 + floor(bond/25)/100), floored.
  - **Damage:**
    - Accuracy uses the accuracy and evasion stages. Physical moves use atk/def and special moves use spa/spd, with stage multipliers.
    - base = floor(floor(floor(2L/5+2)·power·A/D)/50)+2.
    - Then × STAB 1.5 × type multiplier × crit 2 (1/16 chance, or 1/4 with `effect.high_crit`) × random 0.85..1.
    - The result is at least 1 unless immune. `fixed_damage` (a number or "level") overrides.
  - **Catch:** a = (3 − 2·hp_frac)·catch_rate·ball_mult/3 × status_mult (sleep/freeze 2; paralysis/burn/poison 1.5); chance = clamp(a/255, 0, 1).
  - **Exp:** the 6 growth curves; `exp_gain = floor(base_exp·L/7·(1+bond/510))`; `level_for_exp` is capped at 100.
  - **wild_level(d, is_night, rng):** base = 2 + floor(d/80); jitter ±2 from rng; night ×1.2; clamp 2..70.
- **Instance:** as in the contract. `uid` is `"<species_id>-<rng int 1..1e9>"`. The moves are the last 4 distinct learnset moves at or below the level, with PP from move data.
- **Events:** a simple registry; `fire` calls each handler in pcall and logs errors with `core.log` (in the glue; keep events.lua pure by injecting a logger).
