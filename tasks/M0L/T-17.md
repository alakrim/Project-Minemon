# T-17 — Species, moves and types data for all 251 Kanto + Johto Pokémon
ID / milestone / wave / risk: T-17 / M0 / wave 1 / medium (data contract for every later milestone)

## Goal and why
Every creature, fight and capture is data-driven (PLAN invariant 4). We need complete Gen 2 data for dex 1–251, plus our own design fields, as JSON that Lua loads with `core.parse_json`.

## Read
CLAUDE.md, docs/CONVENTIONS.md, docs/research/S-11-creatures-pipeline.md (the PokeAPI CSV list), and the existing data/species.json, data/moves.json and data/types.json. These are 11-species samples from the Godot prototype; keep their schema and extend it.

## Interfaces / invariants
- **Output files:** `mods/minemon/minemon_core/data/species.json`, `moves.json`, `types.json`, `items.json`. Schema: the same as the existing data/*.json (see their keys), and:
  - **species:**
    - `id`, `name`, `types` (1–2), `base_stats{hp,atk,def,spa,spd,spe}`, `catch_rate`, `growth_rate`, `base_exp`, `gender_ratio`;
    - `evolutions[{to, conditions{min_level?, item?, held_item?, time_of_day?, min_bond?, stat_relation?, trade?}}]`;
    - `learnset[{level, move}]`: Gen 2, version groups gold-silver (3) and crystal (4) merged, level-up only, all levels;
    - `temperament`: aggressive | territorial | skittish | peaceful;
    - `size_class`: tiny | small | medium | large | huge | colossal;
    - `body_plan`: biped | quadruped | serpent | avian | blob | insect | fish | other;
    - `habitats`: from the PokeAPI habitat plus our biome tags: plains, forest, mountain, cave, water, sea, beach, desert, snow, swamp, urban, rare;
    - `eldritch`: bool;
    - `height_m`, `weight_kg`.
  - **moves:** every move referenced by any learnset, with Gen 2 values (power, accuracy, PP, type: use move_changelog.csv to roll back to the Gen 2 value), the Gen 2 category by type, and our fields `archetype` (projectile | beam | melee_arc | area_burst | aura | status_cloud), `range_m`, `cast_s`, `cooldown_s` and `effect{...}`, using the same key set as the existing moves.json.
  - **types:** the Gen 2 chart, 17 types.
  - **items:** poke_ball, great_ball, ultra_ball, master_ball, potion, super_potion, link_cable, and the evolution stones (fire, water, thunder, leaf, moon, sun), plus `king_s_rock`, `metal_coat` and `dragon_scale` with catch multipliers where relevant.
- **Design fields are derived by documented rules** in `tools/extract_pokeapi.py`, with per-species overrides in `tools/design_overrides.json`. Examples:
  - temperament from egg group, type and base attack vs defence, with aggressive for e.g. Rattata, Spearow, Ekans, Mankey, Gyarados, Houndour, Sneasel, Tyranitar, and peaceful for e.g. Chansey, Miltank, Togepi, Slowpoke;
  - size_class from height (tiny < 0.4 m, small < 0.9, medium < 1.6, large < 3, huge < 6, colossal ≥ 6);
  - body_plan from the PokeAPI shape;
  - `eldritch = true` for Gengar, Haunter, Gastly, Onix, Steelix, Gyarados, Lapras, Dragonite, Snorlax, Wailord (absent in Gen 2, skip), Lugia, Ho-Oh, Mewtwo, Misdreavus, Unown and Tyranitar.

  Document each rule at the top of the script.

## Steps
1. `tools/extract_pokeapi.py`: downloads the needed CSVs from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/master/data/v2/csv/` into `.tools/cache/pokeapi/` once (urllib, stdlib only) and writes the JSON deterministically (sorted keys, 1-space indent, or compact; your choice, but stable).
2. `tools/tests/test_data.py` (unittest): exactly 251 species, ids 1–251; every type is valid; every learnset move exists in moves.json; every evolution target is in 1–251; catch_rate in 3..255; and spot checks:
   - Raticate base stats 55/81/60/50/70/97;
   - Gengar catch rate 45;
   - Scary Face accuracy 90 (Gen 2);
   - Cyndaquil's learnset contains ember at 12;
   - Eevee has 5 evolutions with Espeon and Umbreon needing bond plus time_of_day;
   - Tyrogue has 3 evolutions with stat_relation;
   - Steelix comes from Onix by trade plus held item metal_coat.
3. Delete the old root `data/` folder (`git rm -r data/`): the files now live under minemon_core/data.
4. `tests/integration/data/`: a Luanti test mod that loads the 4 JSON files with `core.parse_json` from `core.get_modpath("minemon_core") .. "/data/"` and asserts 251 species and non-empty moves. If minemon_core has no init.lua in your tree, read the files by path relative to the test mod using `core.get_modpath`; note this.

## Out of scope / files not to touch / permitted dependencies

2. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tools/tests -v` passes, with at least 12 tests.
3. Report: counts (species, moves, aggressive/peaceful/territorial/skittish split, eldritch count) and 5 spot-check values with their CSV source.
