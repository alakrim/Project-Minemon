# S-03 Prior art and design

## Findings
- **Alien: Isolation:** a "menace" gauge rises with proximity and sight, then the director pulls the threat away. It never cheats, so players feel outwitted.
- **Legends: Arceus:** wild behaviour types (ignore, skittish, aggressive, unaware); sneaking is the counterplay. Critics found its threat unusually unsettling.
- **Palworld:** capture odds (HP, sphere, status) are opaque; players distrust some items that seem to lower them.
- **Mainline catch formula:** HP fraction, species rate, ball and status feed a random roll. Readable and skill-aware.
- **The Long Dark wolves:** sight, sound and scent detection; a warning pose at about 15 m. Fair predator design.
- **Valheim:** random events silently failed until patched. Test every trigger.
- **PS1-style horror:** fog, short draw distance and scratchy audio let imagination fill gaps.
- **Monster Hunter, Don't Starve:** readable early attacks; a learning phase with frequent, cheap deaths.

## Recommendation
Give each creature a visible menace state and telegraphed wind-ups. Show capture odds. Use fog plus sound for low-end dread. Reveal giants in fragments.

## Confidence
High: Alien, Long Dark, Palworld, catch formula. Medium: Left 4 Dead director (secondary source). Low: eldritch scale (no sources; judgement).
