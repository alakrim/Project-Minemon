# S-03 Idea bank

Tags: [core] needed for a complete game, [later] nice to have.

## Core loop
- Explore, meet, read its state, fight in real time, then capture or leave. [core]
- Every creature shows a readable state: calm, wary or hunting. [core]
- Day/night changes which creatures roam and how dangerous trips are. [core]
- Party grows through battles and daily play, not only captures. [core]
- Each trip ends in a safe town with a new reason to go out. [core]

## Combat & difficulty
- Early rat-tier enemy: fast, bites hard, ambushes from grass, flees when hurt. [core]
- Every attack has a clear wind-up pose the player can dodge. [core]
- Stamina gates dodging and sprinting; escape is always possible. [core]
- Level-gap colour warning before engaging a creature. [core]
- Packs of 2-4 at night; ambush only when the player is unaware. [later]
- Losing a fight costs items and time, never the save. [later]

## Living world
- Creatures keep schedules: feed, drink, sleep, return home. [core]
- Territories: a creature chases, then returns to its patch. [core]
- Creatures react to noise, light, fire and carried meat. [core]
- Weather shifts which species appear and how bold they are. [later]
- Migration waves move species across Kanto and Johto over weeks. [later]
- Predators hunt prey creatures and leave tracks to read. [later]
- Random events (storms, hordes) with trigger tests in QA. [later]

## Dread & navigation
- Short sight lines: fog or dusk, explained in-world. [core]
- Sound first: calls, footsteps and rustling before anything is seen. [core]
- Every threat telegraphs: bark, posture or shadow before the strike. [core]
- Long quiet stretches, then one strong encounter. [core]
- Always a route to flee or hide from any threat. [core]
- Torches and fuel run low, forcing a town return. [later]
- Scale tells: bent trees, cold tint, vanishing landmarks. [later]

## Eldritch giants
- Giants appear as fragments first: a shadow, a sound, a footprint. [core]
- Giants ignore the player until the player stands in their path. [core]
- Reserve giants for late game, with a unique trigger site. [core]
- Wrong proportions: too many joints, a head that never turns. [later]
- Fleeing is the first lesson; later, a retreat-and-return fight. [later]
- Low-poly models with jitter and flicker to hint at wrongness. [later]

## Capture & bonding
- Show catch odds as a bar: HP, ball tier and status. [core]
- Ball tiers trade cost for odds; choosing the right one is skill. [core]
- Weakening, sleep and freeze raise odds, as in the mainline pattern. [core]
- Aggressive creatures refuse capture until defeated. [core]
- Bonding grows with feeding, play and battles fought together. [core]
- Small XP from play, larger XP from wins. [core]
- Timed throw aim gives a bonus; a miss is clearly a loss. [later]

## Towns & missions
- Each town has a mission chain tied to its local creatures and lore. [core]
- Missions create reasons to take dangerous routes. [core]
- Healing and shops are calm, lit safe zones. [core]
- Region-leader fights close each Kanto and Johto arc. [later]
- Later missions turn fearful: haunted towers, night escorts. [later]

## Low-end-friendly atmosphere tricks
- Fog and a short draw distance, with detail culled beyond it. [core]
- Limited palette and vertex-colour lighting instead of real shadows. [core]
- Layered ambient audio, directional 3D cues. [core]
- Creature LOD: distant ones become silhouettes or sounds. [core]
- Night screen grade: vignette, grain and muted colour. [later]
