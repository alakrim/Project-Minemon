## Recommendation
- Base: Cobblemon Official Modpack (Fabric 1.21.1, Cobblemon 1.8.1).
- Map: the Cobblemon Johto world.
- Add-ons: Fight or Flight Reborn, Server-Side Horror, plus raid or size mods if needed.
- We write ourselves: lives, the eldritch tier, a wild-aggression rule tied to having no Pokémon out, and extra missions.
